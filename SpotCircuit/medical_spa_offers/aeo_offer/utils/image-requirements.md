# Image Requirements for AEO Landing Page

## Image Specifications
- Minimum resolution: 2000x1200px (can be cropped as needed)
- Format: .jpg for photos, .png for graphics with transparency
- Color space: sRGB
- Max file size: 500KB after optimization

## Required Images by Section

### 1. Hero Section
- **Primary Hero Image**
  - Content: Modern medical spa treatment room with high-end equipment
  - Style: Bright, clean, professional
  - Dimensions: 2000x1200px (16:9 ratio)
  - Source: Shutterstock #2189453692 or similar
  - Priority: Critical

### 2. Equipment Optimization Section
- **Equipment Close-up**
  - Content: Detail shot of aesthetic equipment interface/controls
  - Style: Technical, focused, clean
  - Dimensions: 1200x1200px (1:1 ratio)
  - Source: Adobe Stock #458921367 or similar
  - Priority: High

### 3. Benefits Section
- **ROI Visualization**
  - Content: Before/after treatment setup or data visualization
  - Style: Professional, clinical
  - Dimensions: 1600x900px (16:9 ratio)
  - Source: iStock #897654321 or similar
  - Priority: Medium

### 4. Trust Section
- **Staff Operation Shot**
  - Content: Professional operating/maintaining equipment
  - Style: Action shot, engaged
  - Dimensions: 1200x800px (3:2 ratio)
  - Source: Shutterstock #3456789012 or similar
  - Priority: High

### 5. Consultation Section
- **Consultation Scene**
  - Content: Medical professional with client discussing treatment
  - Style: Warm, professional, engaging
  - Dimensions: 1600x1067px (3:2 ratio)
  - Source: Adobe Stock #567890123 or similar
  - Priority: Medium

## Supporting Images
